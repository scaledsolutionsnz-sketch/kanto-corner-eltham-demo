Eltham, Taranaki

A proper local card shop

Kanto Corner grew out of Eltham and became the town's home for the Pokémon TCG. In January the shop moved into bigger premises at 53 Bridge Street, and a second Kanto Corner has since opened in New Plymouth.

Sheldon and the team keep it simple: fresh stock, straight answers and a counter where you can talk cards as long as you like.

  • Taranaki owned and run
  • Stores in Eltham and New Plymouth
  • Cards, collectibles and game nights under one roof
